VeggieConnect — Order Lifecycle & Pay-After-Approval Gate
Buyers cannot pay before the DA Admin approves an order, and live transport refuses unpaid hauls
Phase 1 · Browse, Cart & Request (Buyer)
1BUYERBrowse the market and farm directory; product cards offer “Add to Cart” only
2BUYERCheckout enforces both minimums — 60 kg total order and 20 kg per product
3BUYERRaise purchase requests from the Cart; the “Choose a vegetable” list is built live from the market menu
↓
Phase 2 · Order Placement & Admin Review
4SYSTEMOrder is stored (MySQL orders) with totals, delivery fee and payment method
5ADMINDA Admin reviews the order in Buyer Requests; buyer sees “Awaiting Admin approval”
↓
Phase 3 · Pay-After-Approval Payment Gate
6BUYEROnce approved, the Orders screen shows “Order approved — pay to start the transaction”
7BUYERPay Now from the VeggieConnect Pay wallet → order marked PAID ✓ WALLET; wallet history records the payment
↓
Phase 4 · Live Transport (Admin-Controlled)
8ADMINAdmin advances the haul in Live Transport; the system refuses unpaid orders (“Buyer payment required”)
9ADMINTransport job tracks pickup farm → drop-off location with estimated weight and driver earnings
↓
Phase 5 · Completion & Settlement
10SYSTEMCompleted transactions reduce warehouse stock automatically
11ADMINUnavailable product lines → “Mark Unavailable & Return Amount” refunds the buyer wallet while the order continues
12BUYERBuyer rates the order; farmer ledger groups sales by date with daily totals
